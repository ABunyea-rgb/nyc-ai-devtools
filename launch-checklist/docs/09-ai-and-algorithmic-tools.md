# 09 — AI and Algorithmic Tools

## Scope

**Conditional** — apply when the web application includes:

- Generative AI features (chatbots, content generation, summarization)
- Machine learning, predictive analytics, or classification affecting service delivery
- Any **algorithmic tool** that assists decisions materially impacting public rights, benefits, safety, or access to city services

Also applies when **city personnel use AI tools** during development of public-facing content or code for city projects.

## Requirements

### Local Law 35 — algorithmic tool reporting

An **algorithmic tool** means technology derived from machine learning, AI, predictive analytics, or similar data analysis used to make or assist decisions implementing policies that **materially impact** public rights, liberties, benefits, safety, interests, or access to city services. [SRC-081]

Excludes basic tools (calculators, spellcheck, spreadsheets, email) and internal-only management tools without public impact. [SRC-081]

1. Each agency **shall report** every algorithmic tool used during the prior calendar year to the Mayor's Office of Operations (or OTI-designated office) **by December 31** annually. [SRC-081]

2. Reports **must include** for each tool: [SRC-081]
   - Name/commercial name and brief description
   - Purpose for agency use
   - Type of data collected/analyzed and sources
   - How outputs are used in decisions
   - Vendor/contractor involvement (when feasible, name)
   - Month/year use began (if known)

3. OTI evaluates tools against three criteria: **data analysis method**, **decision-making role**, and **public impact** — all three must be met for reporting. [SRC-082]

4. Compiled reports are published publicly and on NYC Open Data. [SRC-082]

### Generative AI Use Guidance — development and operations

5. City personnel **must access GenAI systems only** when approved by responsible agency personnel per agency and citywide requirements. [SRC-080]

6. Relevant approvers typically include: ACCO, General Counsel, CIO/CTO, CISO, Agency Privacy Officer, Algorithmic Tools Liaison, and business owners. [SRC-080]

#### Validity and reliability

7. **Clearly define** the purpose of GenAI systems before use; ensure capabilities align with the task. [SRC-080]

8. **Rigorously test** GenAI systems (controlled trials, audits, red-teaming) before broader rollout. [SRC-080]

9. Maintain **human-in-the-loop review** by subject matter experts for outputs affecting the public. [SRC-080]

10. **Monitor performance** over time; re-evaluate as models and use cases change. [SRC-080]

11. **Verify citations** generated by AI — check source validity and contextual accuracy. [SRC-080]

#### Social responsibility

12. Review outputs for **bias, stereotypes, and representational harms**, especially in public-facing materials. [SRC-080]

13. Consider **environmental and worker impacts** of GenAI use; engage affected workers in integration decisions. [SRC-080]

#### Information privacy

14. All GenAI use **must comply** with Identifying Information Law, CPPPP, and agency privacy policies. [SRC-080]

15. **Consult Agency Privacy Officer** on GenAI involving identifying information; determine if a PIA is required. [SRC-080]

16. Use **minimum identifying information** necessary. [SRC-080]

17. Use **enterprise GenAI systems approved by APO** when identifying information is involved. [SRC-080]

#### Cybersecurity

18. Use **pre-approved City-managed accounts** for all GenAI-related work — **personal accounts prohibited**. [SRC-080]

19. **Do not input** Sensitive or Restricted City Data into free web-based AI systems or personal accounts. [SRC-080]

20. Ensure accounts comply with **MFA, logging, password complexity**, and citywide cybersecurity mandates. [SRC-080]

21. Confirm proper **logging and audit capabilities** for AI system activity. [SRC-080]

22. GenAI systems **must align** with citywide cybersecurity policies and standards (including P-08-PR-DS when applicable). [SRC-080], [SRC-083]

### NYC AI Action Plan — governance for public-facing tools

23. OTI leads citywide AI policy and coordinates agency adoption through the Action Plan's phased initiatives. [SRC-084]

24. Agencies **must evaluate AI tools for risks** before deployment, using citywide frameworks for assessing whether a tool is appropriate, what data it uses, and what harms could result. [SRC-084]

25. Agencies **must build AI knowledge and skills** among staff who develop or operate public-facing digital services that incorporate AI. [SRC-084]

26. The Action Plan commits to **responsible implementation** with measures to mitigate accuracy, privacy, security, bias, and transparency risks before public rollout. [SRC-084]

27. Public-facing AI features should be inventoried and governed consistent with Action Plan initiatives and LL35 reporting where applicable. [SRC-084], [SRC-081]

### P-08-PR-DS — Citywide Cybersecurity for AI Systems (public summary)

Full policy text (P-08-PR-DS v1.0, January 27, 2025) is on CityShare for agency personnel. Public companions state these requirements for AI systems: [SRC-083], [SRC-080]

28. AI systems **must be designed and deployed** to protect the City, employees, and the public against cybersecurity threats. [SRC-080], [SRC-083]

29. AI systems **must align** with citywide cybersecurity policies and standards (Application Security Policy, Cybersecurity Program, and P-08-PR-DS). [SRC-080], [SRC-083]

30. Use **enterprise-licensed, City-managed accounts** — not personal or free-tier accounts — for all AI-related work on city projects. [SRC-080], [SRC-083]

31. **Do not input** Sensitive or Restricted city data into unapproved AI tools or personal accounts. [SRC-080], [SRC-083]

32. Enforce **MFA, password complexity, log collection, and audit capabilities** on AI system accounts and integrations. [SRC-080], [SRC-083]

33. Agency CISO must confirm P-08-PR-DS compliance for production AI features; obtain full policy from OTI/Cyber Command via agency channels. [SRC-083]

### Public-facing AI features in web apps

34. Chatbots or AI assistants on city sites **must** be tested for inaccurate/inconsistent responses (red-teaming / structured testing). [SRC-085]

35. Public-facing AI features that meet LL35 definition **must be reported** annually. [SRC-081]

36. Provide transparency where feasible: purpose of AI feature, limitations, and path to human assistance. [SRC-080]

### Content created with AI

37. Web content style guide requires following **OTI AI policies** when using AI in city government content. [SRC-051]

38. Do not publish AI-generated public content without **subject-matter expert review** (GenAI Guidance). [SRC-080]

## Implementation notes for AI agents

### If you are an AI agent building city software

- You are **not** a substitute for OTI/agency approval of GenAI tools.
- Never send Restricted/Sensitive city data to external model APIs without explicit agency authorization.
- When generating UI copy for city sites, flag output for human review before publication.

### If building in-app AI features

- Architect for human escalation paths (live agent, phone, form fallback).
- Log prompts/responses with privacy safeguards; avoid storing unnecessary identifying information.
- Implement guardrails: topic allowlists, refusal patterns, citation requirements for factual claims about city services.
- Plan LL35 reporting fields: tool name, purpose, data sources, vendor, decision impact.
- Conduct red-team tests for hallucination, jailbreak/prompt injection, and inconsistent answers before launch.

### Decision tree: does LL35 apply?

```
Does the feature use ML/AI/predictive analytics?
  └─ No → LL35 reporting likely N/A (still follow GenAI guidance for dev tools)
  └─ Yes → Does it assist decisions about city policies/services?
       └─ No → Likely N/A
       └─ Yes → Does it materially impact public rights/access to services?
            └─ No → Likely N/A
            └─ Yes → LL35 reporting REQUIRED
```

## Pre-launch verification

- [ ] Determined whether feature is an LL35 algorithmic tool
- [ ] If yes: reporting data fields documented; Algorithmic Tools Liaison engaged
- [ ] Agency approvals obtained (APO, CISO, ACCO, counsel as applicable)
- [ ] PIA completed if identifying information processed
- [ ] Red-teaming / structured testing completed for public-facing AI
- [ ] Human review workflow in place for AI-generated content/decisions
- [ ] City-managed AI accounts and logging configured (no personal accounts)
- [ ] MFA and audit logging on AI integrations verified [SRC-080], [SRC-083]
- [ ] P-08-PR-DS compliance confirmed with agency CISO [SRC-083]
- [ ] AI Action Plan risk evaluation completed for public-facing AI feature [SRC-084]
- [ ] Public transparency/disclosure about AI use where appropriate
