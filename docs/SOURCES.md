## Umbrella and Domain

### [SRC-001] Public Digital Experiences Policy
- **Issuing body:** OTI Strategic Initiatives, Digital Service
- **Version:** 1.0 (10/31/2025)
- **URL:** https://designsystem.nyc.gov/standards/policies/public-digital-experiences-policy.html
- **Scope:** Umbrella requirements for new/redesigned public-facing websites, apps, and digital services; NYC Design System adoption; based on 21st Century IDEA

### [SRC-002] nyc.gov Domain Usage Policy
- **Issuing body:** OTI
- **URL:** https://designsystem.nyc.gov/standards/policies/nyc-gov-domain-usage.html
- **Scope:** Domain assignment, HTTPS, prohibited uses, cross-policy compliance (performance, privacy, content, security)

### [SRC-003] NYC Charter Chapter 48 / Executive Order 3 of 2022
- **Issuing body:** City of New York
- **URL:** Authority cited in [SRC-001] and [SRC-020]
- **Scope:** OTI authority over citywide IT, security, privacy, telecommunications

## Accessibility

### [SRC-010] Local Law 26 of 2016
- **Issuing body:** NYC Council
- **URL:** https://www.nyc.gov/assets/mopd/downloads/pdf/Getting-Started-with-Web-Accessibility-December-2021.pdf
- **Scope:** Mandates city website accessibility standards and biennial reporting (Admin Code §23-802)

### [SRC-011] NYC Digital Design System — Accessibility
- **Issuing body:** OTI / NYC Digital Service
- **URL:** https://designsystem.nyc.gov/standards/accessibility.html
- **Scope:** WCAG 2.2 Level AA city standard; 28 CFR § 35.200 federal floor (WCAG 2.1 AA)

### [SRC-012] MOPD Accessible NYC 2025 Report
- **Issuing body:** OTI and MOPD
- **URL:** https://www.nyc.gov/site/mopd/publications/accessiblenyc-2025-report-other-mopd.page
- **Scope:** Fifth biennial report; formal adoption of WCAG 2.2 Level AA for city websites and mobile apps

### [SRC-013] Local Law 12 of 2023
- **Issuing body:** NYC Council
- **URL:** https://www.nyc.gov/assets/acs/pdf/about/2023/local-law-12.pdf
- **Scope:** Five-year agency accessibility plans; web accessibility statements; complaint mechanisms

### [SRC-014] Creating Accessible Content
- **Issuing body:** OTI Web Operations
- **URL:** https://www.nyc.gov/site/process/content/creating-accessible-content.page
- **Scope:** Operational WCAG 2.2 AA guidance for NYC.gov content (titles, contrast, links, tables, accordions)

### [SRC-015] MOPD Getting Started with Web Accessibility
- **Issuing body:** MOPD
- **URL:** https://www.nyc.gov/assets/mopd/downloads/pdf/Getting-Started-with-Web-Accessibility-December-2021.pdf
- **Scope:** Introductory guide; Local Law 26; WCAG principles; content types requiring accessibility

### [SRC-016] MOPD Digital Accessibility Resources
- **Issuing body:** MOPD
- **URL:** https://www.nyc.gov/site/mopd/initiatives/digital-accessibility.page
- **Scope:** City accessibility commitment; Digital Accessibility Coordinator role; feedback form
